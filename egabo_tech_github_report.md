#### 💊 MedSwift — Pharmaceutical Intelligence Engine
**Repo:** [github.com/EGABO-TECH/MedSwift](https://github.com/EGABO-TECH/MedSwift)  
**Language:** Vanilla JavaScript (PWA)  
**Stars:** —  
**Deployed on:** Vercel  

> *"Scan the Truth. Secure Your Health."*

MedSwift is an **enterprise-grade Multimodal Pharmaceutical Intelligence PWA** that turns a mobile camera into a medical auditing tool. It identifies drugs, verifies their authenticity, and extracts clinical intelligence in real-time — all critical for healthcare workers in low-resource settings.

**Intelligence Architecture — Triple-Tier Resilience:**
1. **Stage 1 — OCR Pre-processor**: Extracts text from drug packaging using the `OCR.space` engine
2. **Stage 2 — Tiered AI Reasoning**:
   - **Tier 1 (Sentinel)**: Gemini 2.0 Flash (ultra-fast real-time analysis)
   - **Tier 2 (Expert)**: Gemini 1.5 Pro (complex/degraded label reasoning)
   - **Tier 3 (Safety Net)**: OpenRouter (Claude/Gemini) for ultimate redundancy
3. **Stage 3 — Global Dataset Cross-Referencing**: Queries openFDA, EMA, Health Canada DPD, DrugBank, ChEMBL, and WHO Essential Medicines List

**Key Capabilities:**
- **MedSwift Vision™**: Zero-shot drug identification from live video or image uploads
- **Truth Report™**: Automated clinical cards detailing identity, authenticity, indications
- **Audit-Ready PDF Reports**: Exportable official documentation
- **Offline-first PWA**: Works in low-connectivity African healthcare settings

**Why it matters:** This directly addresses a real African healthcare crisis — counterfeit and unverified medicines. The architecture shows sophisticated understanding of multi-model AI orchestration, regulatory datasets, and resilience engineering. Gemini AI is at its core.
